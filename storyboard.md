# Storyboard: The Last Tree on Earth
Format: 1080x1920 (9:16) | Total: 30s | 6 scenes x 5s

## Music (whole video)
Slow ambient synth pad with a soft, rising cello or piano motif.
Starts quiet, swells at 0:25, fades out by 0:30. Keep it 12-15 dB below the voice.

## Scene 1 | 0:00-0:05 | HOOK
Narration: "Every forest on Earth is gone. Except one."
Visual: Dead grey wasteland, skeletal tree stumps, orange dust haze.
Camera: Slow push-in over the desolate ground.
SFX: Low wind, distant metallic creaks.
Edit: Cut to black for 4 frames on "Except one."

## Scene 2 | 0:05-0:10
Narration: "Sealed beneath a dome of glass, the last tree still breathes."
Visual: Huge glass dome in the wasteland, a single tree glowing inside.
Camera: Slow upward tilt from the ground to the dome.
SFX: Soft electrical hum, gentle exhale-like whoosh.

## Scene 3 | 0:10-0:15
Narration: "Its leaves glow with a light no machine can copy."
Visual: Extreme close-up of bioluminescent teal-gold leaves, drifting light particles.
Camera: Macro slow drift left to right.
SFX: Soft shimmering chimes, faint pulse.

## Scene 4 | 0:15-0:20
Narration: "One scientist crosses the ash, carrying a single seed."
Visual: Lone figure in a worn protective suit walking toward the dome, seed glowing faintly in a gloved hand.
Camera: Wide shot, then slow dolly behind the figure.
SFX: Crunching footsteps on ash, wind rising.

## Scene 5 | 0:20-0:25
Narration: "She plants it in the dust, and waits."
Visual: Close-up of hands pressing the seed into cracked soil, a faint green glow beneath.
Camera: Static low angle, gentle focus pull to the glow.
SFX: Soft thud, silence, one held low tone.

## Scene 6 | 0:25-0:30 | CLOSER
Narration: "The last tree on Earth was never the end. It was the beginning."
Visual: Tiny green sprout breaks the soil; camera pulls back to reveal the dome and the sprout under a clearing sky.
Camera: Smooth pull-back to a wide shot.
SFX: Music swell, a single bright chime on "beginning."
Edit: Hold the final frame 0.5s, fade to black.
